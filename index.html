A Timeline of Removals and Related Deals

Every arrangement to transfer people to a country they are not from, concluded since January 2025, plotted by region and date, with the visa, aid and security moves around them. Open a region to see its countries one line each; click a country for its card, or any item for the agreement, its terms, its recorded flights and its sources. Every flight and the diplomatic meetings around the deals are one chip away; the meetings are machine-extracted and unreviewed.
